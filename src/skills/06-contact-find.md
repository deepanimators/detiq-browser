When finding contact information:
1. Check the company's official website: /contact, /team, /about pages
2. Search LinkedIn for the person's profile
3. Try common email patterns if domain is known: firstname@domain.com, first.last@domain.com
4. Check their social media profiles (Twitter/X, LinkedIn) for contact in bio
5. Use Hunter.io or Apollo search pattern if direct search fails: `site:linkedin.com {name} {company}`
6. Never guess or fabricate contact information — only report what you find
