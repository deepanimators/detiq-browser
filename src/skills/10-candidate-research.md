When researching a candidate:
1. Start with their LinkedIn profile if available
2. Extract: current role, past experience (last 5 years), education, skills, location
3. Search their name + niche for additional context (GitHub, portfolio, publications)
4. Note red flags: unexplained gaps, role mismatch, vague descriptions
5. Score on a simple rubric: experience fit / skill match / culture signals
6. Summarize as a 1-page brief: key facts, fit assessment, suggested interview questions
