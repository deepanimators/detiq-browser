When researching a company:
1. Search for the company's official website first
2. Extract: founding year, HQ location, employee count, funding/revenue if public
3. Search LinkedIn for key people (CEO, founders, leadership team)
4. Check recent news: search `{company name} news {current year}`
5. Find their tech stack on BuiltWith or similar if relevant
6. Summarize: what they do, who they serve, their market position, notable facts
7. Note any red flags or risks if relevant to the user's goal
